## Space Trader (mechanical heritage)

Skiff Run’s trade-run systems are **inspired by and mechanically based on** the classic Palm OS game **Space Trader** by **Pieter Spronck**, released as freeware under the **GNU General Public License (GPLv2)**.

- Author site: https://www.spronck.net/spacetrader/
- Historical source tree: https://github.com/historicalsource/spacetrader

Space Trader itself cites inspiration from SolarWars / DopeWars lineage and Elite (without Elite’s 3D flight). Original Space Trader artwork (except the icon) was copyright Alexander Lawrence — Skiff uses original of1 hull art, not that artwork.
